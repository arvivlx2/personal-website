## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, technical, and deeply credible aesthetic tailored for an Engineer & AI Solutions Developer bridging biopharmaceutical engineering and biomedical strategy. The brand tone is authoritative yet innovative, communicating clinical rigor, computational power, and scientific clarity.

The visual style blends **Technical Minimalism** with **Refined Frosted Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, contemplative dark canvas reminiscent of advanced laboratory consoles, high-throughput compute monitors, and terminal environments.
- **Precision Detailing:** Hairline geometric borders, subtle directional light bleeds, micro-dot matrices, and strict alignment grids project engineering discipline.
- **Biomedical & AI Fusion:** High-voltage cyan/electric blue communicates compute capacity and algorithmic intelligence, while surgical emerald green signals biological vitality, chemical safety, and positive outcomes.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires uncompromised trust, strategic confidence, executive polish, and cutting-edge technical mastery.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated dark-mode hierarchy designed for long analytical sessions, high information density, and focused technical contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Backdrops:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#0B0F17` (Deepest canvas layer)
  - Surface Panel: `#111827` (Base container tone)
  - Elevated Glass: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)` (Composite overlay layer)
- **Structural Lines & Demarcations:**
  - Hairline Subdued: `#1E293B` (Internal compartmentalization and subtle grids)
  - Hairline Interactive: `#334155` (Active borders, separators, hover targets)
  - Luminescent Glow: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)` (Focused, precision stroke states)
- **Chromatic Signals:**
  - **Primary Core (`#38BDF8` / `#06B6D4`):** Electric Sky and Cyan; used for primary interactions, AI workflow state markers, interactive code selections, and high-priority directional indicators.
  - **Secondary Metric (`#10B981`):** Bio-Emerald; applied to validated model runs, regulatory compliance milestones, positive statistical vectors, and successful telemetry.
  - **Text & Contrast:**
    - High-Value Signal: `#F8FAFC` (100% white-slate for titles, values)
    - Body Scientific: `#94A3B8` (Balanced mid-slate for legibility and stamina)
    - Metadata & Low-Signal: `#64748B` (De-emphasized references, line numbers, units)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy integrates three complementary typefaces to articulate domain authority:
1. **Display & Structural Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean geometry, crisp modern terminals, and human-engineered clarity. Headlines under 32px maintain high legibility without feeling sterile.
2. **Analytical Body (Inter):** Neutral, hyper-legible, highly systematic sans-serif built for reading data arrays, research dossiers, and dense technical copy.
3. **Telemetry & Technical Accents (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced precision for chemical formulas, molecular weights, ML architecture hyper-parameters, timestamps, pill metadata, and terminal executions.

Headings must enforce tight, intentional letter-spacing to eliminate visual looseness and project compact, solid form.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an engineering grid: a strictly enforced 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display deep data dashboards alongside technical narrative content.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, max-width bounded at 1440px for comfortable panoramic viewing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Content reflows into side-by-side or stacked technical panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Grid blocks collapse into single-column vertical cards without truncated telemetry.

### Spacing Principles
- Spacing follows an absolute 4px/8px incremental base.
- Generous outer section margins contrast against dense internal component padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`), emphasizing precision machinery surrounded by calm, focused focus areas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use murky drop shadows; instead, it relies on **Luminescent Glassmorphism** paired with **Directional Surface Tiers**.

1. **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep `#0B0F17` textured with an ultra-faint micro-grid pattern (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` 24px mesh).
2. **Layer 1 (Modular Panels):** `#111827` backing with 1px border of `#1E293B`.
3. **Layer 2 (Floating Glassmorphism):** 
   - Background: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.65)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`
   - Border: 1px continuous hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` with an inner top hairline highlight of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`.
4. **Active/Focused Glow Elevation:** Ambient light bleeds: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, casting an ethereal electric halo when executing operations or inspecting models.

## Shapes

The design system uses a strict **Soft (`1`)** corner radius strategy. Rounded corners are restrained to project high-tolerance engineering specs rather than casual consumer apps.

- **Base Controls, Badges, and Text Inputs:** `rounded` (0.25rem / 4px).
- **Glass Cards, Modal Overlays, and Code Panels:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Macro Containment Frameworks:** `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px).
- **Status Indicators & Terminal Dots:** Strictly geometric circles (`rounded-full`).

This controlled curvature gives buttons and cards the look of CNC-machined instruments and precision biomedical cassettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Technical Action:** Filled `#38BDF8` with dark text (`#0B0F17`), font `Inter` semibold. On hover, background shifts to `#06B6D4` with a subtle `0 0 16px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)` glow.
- **Secondary / Analytical:** Transparent background, 1px border `#334155`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, border turns `#38BDF8` and background gains `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.05)`.
- **Destructive / Abort:** Border `#EF4444`, text `#FCA5A5`, dark red tint on interaction.

### Chips & Badges
- Built with `JetBrains Mono` at `label-code` specs.
- Rendered in pill or 4px container formats with an inline colored status beacon (6px dot).
- *Bio/Pharma Variant:* Emerald border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#10B981`, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`.
- *AI/ML Variant:* Electric Cyan border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`, text `#38BDF8`, background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Glassmorphic treatment with `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)` background, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, and 1px crisp outline in `#1E293B`.
- Features an optional corner accent: a 2px micro-bracket or gradient edge indicating active processing or valid compliance.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#0B0F17`, border 1px `#1E293B`, font `Inter` 14px. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- Focused state: 1px border `#38BDF8`, with a zero-offset box-shadow of `0 0 0 1px #38BDF8`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with 2px radius for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Inactive: Border `#334155`, background `#111827`.
- Active: Border `#38BDF8`, background `#38BDF8`, glyph `#0B0F17`.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Micro-bordered rows separated by 1px `#1E293B`.
- Header row set in `JetBrains Mono` uppercase with `#64748B` typography.
- Alternating hover row highlight using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Telemetry Metric Tile:** Large tabular figures, mono label, and inline sparkline showing model convergence or clinical trial assay kinetics.
- **Console Terminal Window:** `#070A0F` dark box, custom slim scrollbar, line numbers in `#334155`, containing executable AI models, SMILES chemical strings, or deployment logs.
